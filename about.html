About Me

I am an AI & Data Engineering professional with an MSc in Artificial Intelligence from the University of Essex, supported by a strong foundation in Mechanical Engineering that provides deep understanding of physical systems, numerical methods, and real-world problem solving. My work focuses on building intelligent, deployable solutions that bridge data, perception, and decision-making, particularly in computer vision, machine learning, automation, and analytics. I have hands-on experience developing end-to-end AI pipelines from data acquisition and preprocessing to model training, evaluation, and deployment using technologies including Python, TensorFlow, PyTorch, YOLOv8, OpenCV, Scikit-Learn, SQL, and Power BI.

My projects emphasize real-time perception and predictive systems, such as developing CNN-LSTM architectures for audio classification and building live object detection pipelines suitable for edge deployment. In addition to academic work, I bring industry experience across manufacturing, logistics, and operations, where I applied data analytics, ERP systems, and KPI-driven planning to improve efficiency, reduce waste, and support strategic decision-making. This interdisciplinary background enables me to approach problems from both technical and operational perspectives.

Technically, my strengths include machine learning, computer vision, data analytics, signal processing, sensor-driven systems, and automation pipelines. Equally important are my non-technical skills: strong analytical thinking, problem solving, communication, teamwork, leadership, adaptability, time management, and a commitment to continuous learning. I thrive in complex environments where solutions require creativity, structured thinking, and collaboration across disciplines.

I am particularly motivated by challenges at the intersection of AI and the physical world, including autonomous systems, environmental monitoring, robotics perception, intelligent sensing, and large-scale data-driven decision systems. Currently based in London with the right to work in the UK, I am open to opportunities in artificial intelligence, data engineering, research collaborations, and funded PhD programs where advanced AI can create meaningful real-world impact.
